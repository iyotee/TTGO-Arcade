TTGO-Arcade

Tigo's pocket arcade for the LilyGO TTGO T-Display

Talk to your board

The companion page connects to the board over Bluetooth: send Tigo messages and treats, give the board your WiFi, set its clock and install the big games.

Open the companion

Chrome or Edge on Android or a computer. On the board: Apps > Companion, then scan its QR code.

What's inside

  • Over 50 two-button games, from falling blocks to pinball
  • Big games (Abyx, Crypt) as a downloadable cartridge
  • Pocket apps: clock, timer, dice, Bluetooth remote...
  • Tigo the hermit crab, who lives in the screensaver
  • Updates over WiFi, straight from GitHub
